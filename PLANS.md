# ExecPlans

Significant Lyra work is executed through a living, self-contained plan in `docs/exec-plans/`.

Each plan contains: Purpose, Scope, Progress, Architecture/Context, Implementation steps, Acceptance criteria, Verification commands, Surprises & Discoveries, Decision Log, and Outcomes & Retrospective. Update progress only after its acceptance checks run. Record assumptions and measured results so a later session can resume without chat history.
